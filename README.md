# CENTER

Notes, PDFs and C coding (with the MSP430 tools for ECE 306) in one calm workspace.

## Download

**[Download CENTER for Windows](https://github.com/Morrow-Dev/CENTER/releases/latest/download/CENTER-Setup.exe)** (Windows 11, 64-bit)

Open **CENTER-Setup.exe**. It installs CENTER for your Windows account (no administrator rights needed), adds it to the
Start menu and your desktop, and keeps it up to date automatically.

> Windows may show a blue **"Windows protected your PC"** screen, because CENTER is new and its installer is not signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Remove CENTER any time from **Settings › Apps**. Your notes, PDFs, projects and settings stay on your computer.

Every version: [Releases](https://github.com/Morrow-Dev/CENTER/releases)

## Check your download

Every release lists the SHA-256 of its installer in its notes and in its **SHA256SUMS** file. In PowerShell, in the
folder you downloaded it to:

    Get-FileHash .\CENTER-Setup.exe

The **Hash** it prints should be exactly the one on the release's page.
