# CENTER

CENTER by Morrow: notes, PDFs and C coding (with the MSP430 tools for ECE 306) in one calm workspace.

## Download

**[Download CENTER for Windows](https://github.com/Morrow-Dev/CENTER/releases/latest/download/CENTER-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

Open **CENTER-Setup.exe**. It installs CENTER for your Windows account (no administrator rights needed), adds it to the
Start menu and your desktop, and keeps it up to date automatically.

> Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"** the first time, because this installer is new. Choose **More info**,
> then **Run anyway**.

Remove CENTER any time from **Settings › Apps**. Your notes, PDFs, projects and settings stay on your computer.

Every version: [Releases](https://github.com/Morrow-Dev/CENTER/releases)
